2 · Pictures (in chronological order)

Rename each picture. Use commas for several trigger words: walter, heisenberg. Click “Insert [tag]” to put the tag in the script.

Timeline Animation (separate tool for chronological scripts)

Timeline Animation — create a timeline video

Automatic: the timeline detects years and dates in your script and uses the surrounding sentence as each event title. It refreshes when the dates in the script change. You can edit the detected events below. Add {timeline} on its own line exactly where the timeline should appear; text before and after keeps its order. Supported examples: 3000 BCE, 500 BC, 1760, 2026 CE.

Design & animation settings (optional)

You can leave these settings unchanged to use the default look.

Example script: Our story begins, blank line, {timeline}, blank line, And the story continues. The timeline section gets its own duration based on your events; all surrounding text stays before or after it in chronological order.
